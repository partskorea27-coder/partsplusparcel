COMPANY OVERVIEW

10 years in the business

Established in 2016, Parts+Parcel has spent 10 years supplying genuine and aftermarket spare parts to workshops, retailers, fleet operators, and dealerships. What started as a single-brand parts counter has grown into a multi-brand distribution operation covering Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Mazda, Hyundai, Kia, Mitsubishi and BYD.

The reason customers stay is unchanged: every part that ships has been checked against the vehicle it's meant to fit. We'd rather tell you a part is a few days out than quote "in stock" and let a workshop find out otherwise on the day of the repair.

As BYD and other newer brands become a bigger part of the vehicle mix on the road, we're expanding our sourcing coverage to keep pace — so the brands you need today are matched by the brands your customers will be driving tomorrow.
